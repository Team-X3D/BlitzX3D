# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in BlitzX3D. This is a solo-maintained project, so
please read this before opening a PR or issue

## AI Policy

I can't police how you write your PRs, nor do I care. But every contribution
must be backed by a human who understands it

- **No AI-generated or agent-opened PRs.** AI co-author tags (`Co-Authored-By:
  Claude`, etc.) and bot PRs will get closed without discussion
- **No LLM-generated issues, review comments, or PR descriptions.** If you
  can't explain it and defend it, don't submit it
- **You must understand every line you submit.** "The AI wrote it" is not an
  acceptable answer to review questions

## Contributions

- Keep PRs focused on one concern and match existing code style
- Build and test your change ([see README](README.md#how-to-build))
- Explain *why*, and not just *what*

I may close any contribution that doesn't appear to be authored by the submitter
